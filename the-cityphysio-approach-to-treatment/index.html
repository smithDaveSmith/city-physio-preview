Our approach

A plan you
can understand.

CityPhysio uses a three-stage approach: assessment, treatment and rehabilitation.

01

Assessment

Understand what’s going on.

Your physiotherapist takes your history and assesses your movement. You discuss the findings, treatment options and likely number of appointments. If appropriate, they arrange onward referral.

02

Treatment

Care shaped around you.

Hands-on treatment may include manual therapy for joints, nerves and muscles, alongside exercises to continue at home or work. Your treatment is guided by the assessment.

03

Rehabilitation

Work towards independence.

A tailored exercise programme addresses the factors contributing to your condition. This may include Pilates and advice on training or workplace ergonomics. The aim is independent self-management.

Clarity comes first.

Your physiotherapist will explain the findings and discuss your treatment. When physiotherapy is not the right next step, they will arrange referral to an appropriate professional.
