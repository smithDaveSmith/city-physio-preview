Pilates classes

Classes run by specialist physiotherapists, aimed at control, posture and core stability.

The purpose is practical: better muscle control and posture reduce uneven wear on tissues and help stop an injury recurring. For many patients Pilates is the third stage of treatment rather than a separate activity.

If you are a CityPhysio patient, you complete a Pilates assessment form with your physiotherapist first. If you are not, arrive ten minutes early for a brief screening with the instructor.

Class times. Class times change. Book through the online booking system — choose Lucan Clinic, then Group Sessions — or ask reception.

Before your first class

  • If you are a CityPhysio patient: complete a Pilates assessment form with your physiotherapist.
  • If you are not: arrive ten minutes early for a brief screening with the instructor.
  • Wear clothing you can move in. Classes are run by specialist physiotherapists.
