Book an appointment

Booking runs on Cliniko, the clinic’s own practice-management system. You do not need a GP referral. Choose Lucan Clinic, then your service — for Pilates, choose Group Sessions.

Live booking system. The panel below is CityPhysio’s real Cliniko booking system, embedded from mjphysioltd-ta-cityphysio.eu1.cliniko.com. Anything you book here is a real appointment at the clinic.

If the booking panel does not load, open it directly at https://mjphysioltd-ta-cityphysio.eu1.cliniko.com/bookings or call (01) 628 0855.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel, or a €50 fee applies.

Health insurance

VHI, Laya Healthcare, Irish Life Health, ESB Insurance, Garda Medical. Logos for these insurers appear on the current site. Cover varies by policy — always check with your insurer before your appointment.

Referral

Self-referral is welcome. We also accept referrals from GPs and consultants.
