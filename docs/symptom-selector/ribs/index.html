Ribs

Rib pain often originates in the joints where the ribs meet the thoracic spine.

Where the thoracic spine is not moving well, rib pain can follow — typically provoked by movement, by coughing, or by breathing deeply.

When not to wait for physiotherapy

Rib and chest pain can have cardiac and respiratory causes as well as musculoskeletal ones. Some symptoms need urgent medical assessment rather than physiotherapy — including loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the groin or inner thighs, weakness in both legs, severe pain following a significant injury, or pain alongside fever or unexplained weight loss. Contact your GP or an emergency department first.

If you are not sure, call us on (01) 628 0855 and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right people to see.

General safety guidance, consistent with HSE and NHS public advice. Not a diagnosis.

What happens if you book

Every appointment starts with assessment, not treatment. Your physiotherapist takes a history, examines you, and then tells you what is wrong, what treatment is needed and roughly how many sessions it should take.
