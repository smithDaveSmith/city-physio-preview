Services

We don’t treat a list of medical conditions. We treat people with aches, pains, injuries and movement problems — through these 9 services.

01

Physiotherapy

We don't treat a list of medical conditions. We treat people with aches, pains, injuries and movement problems.

→ 02

Tendon Clinic

A dedicated programme for tendon problems, built on best-practice guidelines.

→ 03

Shockwave Therapy

Extracorporeal Shock Wave Therapy (ESWT) for chronic and persistent soft-tissue injuries.

→ 04

Sports Physiotherapy

For athletes from club level to international level.

→ 05

Pilates Classes

Classes run by specialist physiotherapists, aimed at control, posture and core stability.

→ 06

Women's Health

Specialist pelvic floor physiotherapy.

→ 07

Acupuncture and Dry Needling

Two distinct needle techniques, used for different purposes.

→ 08

Corporate and On-site Services

On-site physiotherapy and ergonomics for employers. We have been doing this for 15 years.

→ 09

Hospital Services

Physiotherapy provided in a number of private hospitals on a flexible basis.
