Service 01

Physiotherapy

We don't treat a list of medical conditions. We treat people with aches, pains, injuries and movement problems.

Assessment comes first: we work out what is actually wrong and what caused it, then tell you plainly. Care centres on hands-on manual treatment combined with rehabilitation through exercise.

The goal is to get you back to work, back to your sport, or simply out of pain — and to leave you able to manage the problem yourself.

What it involves

  • Hands-on manual therapy
  • Rehabilitation through exercise
  • Self-referral welcome — no GP letter needed
  • Referrals accepted from GPs and consultants
