The clinic and the team

A spacious, modern clinic in the centre of Lucan village, minutes from the N4, N7 and M50.

Treatment is provided only by chartered physiotherapists with advanced qualifications in musculoskeletal and sporting injuries.

Some of the team hold additional qualifications in orthotics prescription, dry needling, women's health and ergonomics.

We accept self-referrals as well as referrals from GPs and consultants, and offer a consultant service to other physiotherapists.

Getting here

Minutes from the N4, N7, M50. Dublin Bus routes 66a, 66b, 67a, 67b, 25 from College Green, Dublin city centre.
